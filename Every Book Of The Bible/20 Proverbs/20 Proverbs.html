20 Proverbs

  1. Proverbs 4

    • 5-6 Get wisdom, get understanding; do not forget my words or turn away from them.

      Do not forsake wisdom, and she will protect you; love her, and she will watch over you.

    • 7-8 The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom. Though it cost all you have, get understanding.

      Cherish her, and she will exalt you; embrace her, and she will honor you.

    • 18-19 The path of the righteous is like the morning sun, shining ever brighter till the full light of day.

      But the way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know what makes them stumble.

    • 20-22 My son, pay attention to what I say; turn your ear to my words.

      Do not let them out of your sight, keep them within your heart;

      for they are life to those who find them and health to one's whole body.

    • 23-24 Above all else, guard your heart, for everything you do flows from it.

      Keep your mouth free of perversity; keep corrupt talk far from your lips.

  2. Proverbs 10

    • 1-5 A wise son brings joy to his father, but a foolish son brings grief to his mother.

      Ill-gotten treasures have no lasting value, but righteousness delivers from death.

      The LORD does not let the righteous go hungry, but he thwarts the craving of the wicked.

      Lazy hands make for poverty, but diligent hands bring wealth.

      He who gathers crops in summer is a prudent son, but he who sleeps during harvest is a disgraceful son.

    • 8 The wise in heart accept commands, but a chattering fool comes to ruin.

    • 12 Hatred stirs up conflict, but love covers over all wrongs.

    • 17 Whoever heeds discipline shows the way to life, but whoever ignores correction leads others astray.

    • 19 Sin is not ended by multiplying words, but the prudent hold their tongues.

    • 19 The fear of the LORD adds length to life, but the years of the wicked are cut short.
