  1. 1 Timothy 1

    • 3-5 As I urged you when I went into Macedonia, stay there in Ephesus so that you may command certain people not to teach false doctrines any longer

      or to devote themselves to myths and endless genealogies. Such things promote controversial speculations rather than advancing God's work—which is by faith.

      The goal of this command is love, which comes from a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith.

    • 15-17 Here is a trustworthy saying that deserves full acceptance: Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners—of whom I am the worst.

      But for that very reason I was shown mercy so that in me, the worst of sinners, Christ Jesus might display his immense patience as an example for those who would believe in him and receive eternal life.

      Now to the King eternal, immortal, invisible, the only God, be honor and glory for ever and ever. Amen.

    • 20 Among them are Hymenaeus and Alexander, whom I have handed over to Satan to be taught not to blaspheme.
