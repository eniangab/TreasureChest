  1. 1 John 2

    • 1-2 My dear children, I write this to you so that you will not sin. But if anybody does sin, we have an advocate with the Father—Jesus Christ, the Righteous One.

      He is the atoning sacrifice for our sins, and not only for ours but also for the sins of the whole world.

    • 4-6 Whoever says, "I know him," but does not do what he commands is a liar, and the truth is not in that person.

      But if anyone obeys his word, love for God is truly made complete in them. This is how we know we are in him:

      Whoever claims to live in him must live as Jesus did.

    • 9-10 Anyone who claims to be in the light but hates a brother or sister is still in the darkness.

      Anyone who loves their brother and sister lives in the light, and there is nothing in them to make them stumble.
